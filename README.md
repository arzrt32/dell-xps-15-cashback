# dell xps 15: What Replaced It, Where to Still Buy One, and How to Cut the Price With Cashback

Search "dell xps 15" and you'll land on a pile of product pages, deals and reviews for a laptop Dell hasn't refreshed in years. Open the manufacturer's own shop and there's an XPS 13, an XPS 14, an XPS 16 — and nothing with 15 inches on it anywhere.

That's not a website glitch. Dell ended the 15-inch XPS, then briefly ended the whole XPS name, then brought the name back without bringing back a 15-inch model. So if you want an XPS 15 today, you're shopping one of two very different markets: leftover or refurbished 15-inch stock, or the machines Dell currently sells in its place. Below is what each actually costs, where the stock still exists, and how the cashback route through ShopBack changes the maths — including the terms that quietly disqualify people from getting paid.

## Dell stopped making the XPS 15, and the new lineup has no replacement

The newest XPS 15 you'll realistically find on sale is the 9530 — Intel 13th-gen chips (i7-13700H up to i9-13900H), DDR5 memory, and either a 1920×1200 non-touch panel or a 3.5K OLED touch screen, with RTX 4050/4060-class graphics in the higher configs.

When Dell refreshed the line, it went straight from 15 inches to 14 and 16. The 16-inch XPS took over the space the XPS 15 used to occupy, and 15 inches disappeared.

Then the naming got strange. In 2025 Dell killed off the XPS badge entirely, folded everything into Dell / Dell Pro / Dell Pro Max, and called the former XPS laptops "Dell 14 Premium" and "Dell 16 Premium." At CES 2026, Dell's COO Jeff Clarke went on stage and said the decision had been a mistake. The XPS name came back, with redesigned 14- and 16-inch models and an XPS 13 promised later in 2026.

No 15-inch XPS is on that roadmap. If you're waiting for one, nothing Dell has said suggests it's coming.

> Worth knowing: those 2025 "Dell 14 Premium" and "Dell 16 Premium" laptops have since been renamed to XPS 14 (2025) and XPS 16 (2025). Identical hardware, different badge. When you see a heavy discount on a "Dell 14 Premium," you're looking at the same machine now sold as an XPS 14 (2025) — and it was discounted hard during the rename.

## What Dell's current XPS line actually is

The 2026 XPS 14 and XPS 16 run Intel's Core Ultra Series 3 "Panther Lake" chips, and the single biggest thing to know is what Dell removed: discrete graphics. The old XPS 15 could be ordered with a dedicated NVIDIA GPU. The new XPS 14 and XPS 16 use integrated Intel Arc graphics only. If your reason for wanting an XPS 15 was rendering, CAD or gaming on the side, the new line doesn't serve that, and Dell's own guidance is to look at Alienware or a workstation instead.

What else changed: physical function keys are back, replacing the capacitive touch row Dell used since 2022. The touchpad is still haptic, but now has faint etched lines so you can see where it starts. Three Thunderbolt 4 ports and a headphone jack — that's the entire port selection, so HDMI or USB-A means a dongle. Wi-Fi 7 and Bluetooth 6.0. An 8MP 4K webcam. A 70Wh battery, and a display that drops to 1Hz when nothing is moving, which is where Dell's battery-life claims come from.

## The terms that decide whether you actually get paid

This is the part most cashback articles skip, and it's the part that costs people money.

**Start from ShopBack, every time.** Cashback only tracks if your visit to Dell begins with a click from the platform, and that click has to be the last link you used. Open the site directly afterwards and the tracking breaks.

**Empty your Dell cart first.** For Dell specifically, ShopBack's own instructions say to clear the cart before clicking through. Items already sitting in your basket when you arrive aren't tracked.

**Skip the Dell app.** Purchases made through Dell's mobile app don't earn cashback, and neither do accessories bought through the Dell app.

**Accept the cookies.** Dell can only confirm an order to ShopBack if you accept all cookies on its pages. Decline them and you can end up with a purchase that never gets recorded.

**Turn off adblockers and VPNs, and don't click other links in between.** Google or Facebook ads, browser extensions, and other cashback tools can all break the session. ShopBack flags these explicitly.

Note what's excluded on Dell: Outlet orders, Dell Refurbished Computers orders, Dell Small Business and Premier orders, employee purchase programs, education, government and enterprise orders, orders placed with a call or chat agent, quote-to-order purchases, price matches and adjustments, preorders, and gift cards. In other words, buying a refurbished XPS 15 to save money gives up the cashback entirely. Do that maths deliberately rather than discovering it 60 days later.

**The payout isn't instant and it isn't always the number you saw.** Purchases typically track within about two days. Confirmation takes far longer — ShopBack's Dell pages have listed 65 days in one market and 120 days in another. There's also a documented behaviour where cashback initially tracks at a different rate and is adjusted later once the transaction details are confirmed. It happens to real buyers: back in 2019, an OzBargain user ordered a roughly $3,700 XPS 15 after clicking through at an advertised 9%, and saw 3% post to their account. Complaints get resolved, but read the rate carefully and keep a screenshot of the page you clicked from.

**Calculation is on the price, not the total you paid.** Taxes, shipping and delivery fees, credit card charges and any amounts paid with store credit are stripped out before the percentage is applied. On a headline 4%, the real return on your out-the-door total is lower than 4%.

**Coupons only stack if ShopBack carries them.** Purchases made with vouchers or promo codes not featured on the platform void the cashback. A Dell discount code you found elsewhere plus cashback is generally a pick-one situation, not a stack.

**Returns kill it.** Rejected, cancelled, refunded, exchanged or returned orders don't earn cashback, and partial returns are prorated as an adjustment.

## Old XPS 15, new XPS 16, or a renamed 2025 Premium?

Three routes, and they suit different buyers.

Buy the older XPS 15 if the 15-inch size, the keyboard, or a dedicated GPU is the actual reason you searched that term. The Best Buy open-box 9530 at $807.99 with an RTX 4050 and 1TB of storage is the cheapest way into a machine with a discrete GPU, and the $1,299.99 OLED i9 config beats the new XPS 16 on memory, storage and graphics while costing less. Accept that you're buying into a discontinued line with no new firmware coming.

Buy the new XPS 14 or XPS 16 if you want a current Core Ultra Series 3 chip, a fresh warranty, a much thinner chassis and the best battery life Dell has put in this line — up to 40 hours claimed on the low-res panel. Confirm that the listing is a new Dell.com purchase and not Outlet stock before assuming cashback applies.
